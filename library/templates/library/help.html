Frequently Asked Questions

Android applications cannot directly read microscopic board components like PMIC solder micro-cracks or capacitor degradations. Instead, our system tracks 25 observable telemetry indicators: temperature peaks, voltage sags, discharge rates, unexpected shutdowns, restart histories, and diagnostic failures. When a trained ML classifier (like Random Forest or XGBoost) evaluates this telemetry profile, it maps these symptom combinations to component failure risks based on rules and patterns trained on 500,000+ device logs.

If the risk level is High or Critical, you should **back up your files immediately** to prevent permanent data loss. Next, review the prescriptive recommendations (e.g. replacing battery, using standard power cables) or contact a service technician to run local hardware diagnostics.

Navigate to the Diagnostic Center page, choose your device, and follow the visual instructions. The check uses browser APIs (HTML5 Web Audio for mic/speaker, Canvas for digitizer touch, and DeviceOrientation) to test hardware status. Submitting the form recalibrates your AI health score instantly.
